Hobbies

🏔 Hiking & Exploring

Hiking

Mountains stand, waters run.

In 2024 I climbed Abuji Tso in Yunnan, China, at an altitude of 4,400 metres. This was my second high altitude trek. In the future, I will continue to challenge myself and my next goal is Haba Snow Mountain at 5300 metres.

In 2025, I completed the Panorama Ridge trail solo in approximately 8 hours. The trail, located near Vancouver, is a 30 km round trip with an elevation gain of about 1,000 metres. Along the way, I encountered a black bear feeding on berries, which was a memorable and slightly intimidating moment. I also spotted a lynx near a lake, as well as a deer further along the trail.

4,400m High altitude trek Next: 5300m / 100km

🌍 Road Trip 🚗

Travel

Wander where the wind blows.

In 2024, my total road trip mileage was about 15,000 kilometres. I travelled the west coast of the US (Vancouver-San Francisco-Los Angeles-San Diego-Las Vegas-Vancouver). In addition, I drove about 4,000 kilometres in Yunnan, China in order to taste different Pu'er teas. I have some other short trips (within 1000km). in 2025, my goal is to drive to Tuktoyaktuk in the north of Canada, in the middle of the Arctic Circle. I also plan to drive the middle of the USA.

2025 end. This year I changed my plan but Tuktoyaktuk is still my dream. It is such a busy year. My graduate program is too busy! In my summer vacation, I went back to west-middle of US from San Francisco to Las Vegas then turn north to Yellowstone National Park. I visited 9 national parks and Salt Lake City.(Totally 5500km) In the next half year. After that, I also had a 1500km small road trip near seattle. In the end of September, driving the Tang Poetry Road from Hangzhou to Wenzhou, I found myself inside Li Bai’s line— “天姥连天向天横，势拔五岳掩赤城.” Some landscapes are not meant to be seen, but to be understood.

2026 is in progress...
